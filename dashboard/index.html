How radar-7 prices a launch

Implied value = TVL × the category multiple × seventeen factors, capped by a live-cap anchor. The multiple is the category's geometric median market-cap / TVL ratio among live-token protocols, shrunk toward the global median when the sample is thin. The anchor caps every row at 1.5× the largest live market cap its category can evidence — the global max when the category holds fewer than three live market caps — so no launch is ever claimed to be worth more than the live market has paid for a peer. New in radar-7: the anchor, category liveliness, early momentum, a drawdown guard, and standing inside the category. No protocol name is ever special-cased. Implied value is a model, not a promise, and never a prediction of whether or when a token is issued. A protocol "records no token" in the snapshot; the record is not proof it has none.
